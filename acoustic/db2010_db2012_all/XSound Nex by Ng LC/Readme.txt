Features:

Nexus™ Engine
- Hear the bass booming inside your ear with the new Nexus™ Engine without even cracking the bass!

ANRD™ 3 Engine
- ANRD™ (Advanced Noise Reduction Data) is back with a newly improved version with much more better noise-cancelling and also added boosting sound quality features! Thanks to it, you will have better music experience, like you are in a concert listening to your favourite songs.

Nexus™ Engine + ANRD™ 3 = HD
- With clear quality music and good bass, take your music to the Nex™ level with Full HD sound quality!

Loud Backspeaker
- With XSound™ Nex™,your backspeaker will sound even louder than any other acoustic and also it includes ANRD™ 3 Backspeaker Edition, which is a new and also the best noise-cancelling and sound boosting engine.

Xsis™ for non-walkman phones
- Using a non-walkman phone? Xsis™ restores the sound effect for the same great music experience,as if you have a Walkman phone.

Good for Listening
- XSound™ Nex™ is good for listening without even hurting your ears.
